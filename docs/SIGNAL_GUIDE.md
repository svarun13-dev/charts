# AstroTrader Signal Interpretation Guide

## Understanding Planetary Aspects

Aspects are angular relationships between planets. Each has a different energy:

### Conjunction (☌) - 0°
**Energy:** Intensification, fusion of energies
**Trading:** Strong momentum in either direction - watch the planets involved
- Jupiter conjunction = expansion, growth potential
- Saturn conjunction = restriction, consolidation
- Mars conjunction = aggressive moves, volatility

### Opposition (☍) - 180°
**Energy:** Tension, polarity, awareness
**Trading:** Often marks turning points or reversals
- Look for exhaustion in trends
- Good for spotting overbought/oversold conditions

### Square (□) - 90°
**Energy:** Friction, challenge, action required
**Trading:** Generally bearish/volatile
- Obstacles and resistance levels
- Can trigger breakdowns
- Often precedes significant moves after tension releases

### Trine (△) - 120°
**Energy:** Harmony, flow, ease
**Trading:** Generally bullish
- Smooth upward movement
- Good for entering long positions
- Support levels tend to hold

### Sextile (⚹) - 60°
**Energy:** Opportunity, potential
**Trading:** Mildly bullish
- Requires action to capitalize
- Good entry points
- Moderate support

## Retrograde Periods

When a planet appears to move backward from Earth's view:

### Mercury Retrograde ⚠️ HIGH ALERT
- **Duration:** ~3 weeks, 3-4x per year
- **Effects:** Communication breakdowns, tech glitches, contract issues
- **Trading:**
  - Avoid major new positions
  - Expect increased volatility
  - Good for reviewing/researching
  - Watch for false breakouts

### Venus Retrograde
- **Duration:** ~6 weeks every 18 months
- **Effects:** Value reassessment, relationship issues
- **Trading:**
  - Review financial commitments
  - Avoid luxury purchases
  - Good for finding undervalued assets

### Mars Retrograde
- **Duration:** ~10 weeks every 2 years
- **Effects:** Frustrated action, delayed results
- **Trading:**
  - Reduce aggressive positions
  - Avoid starting new ventures
  - Good for strategic planning

## Vedic-Specific Concepts

### Nakshatras (Lunar Mansions)
27 divisions of the zodiac, each 13°20'. The Moon's nakshatra affects:
- Daily market mood
- Short-term sentiment
- Emotional trading patterns

### Tithis (Lunar Days)
30 lunar days per month:
- **Shukla Paksha** (Waxing): Generally more favorable for growth
- **Krishna Paksha** (Waning): Better for exits and consolidation

### Key Tithis for Trading:
- **Amavasya** (New Moon): New cycle, potential reversals
- **Purnima** (Full Moon): Peak energy, watch for exhaustion
- **Ekadashi** (11th): Traditionally auspicious

## Example Signal Interpretation

**Signal:** "Jupiter-Uranus Conjunction in Taurus"
- **Jupiter:** Expansion, growth
- **Uranus:** Sudden change, innovation
- **Conjunction:** Intensification
- **Taurus:** Value, stability, finance

**Interpretation:** Major expansion in innovative financial technologies. Could signal sudden growth in crypto or fintech. Watch for unexpected positive developments in value-based assets.

**Action:** Look for long opportunities in crypto and innovative financial instruments, but use stops due to Uranus volatility.

## Best Practices

1. **Don't trade signals alone** - Use as one factor among many
2. **Stronger aspects = more attention** - Focus on conjunctions, oppositions, squares
3. **Outer planet aspects last longer** - Jupiter/Saturn aspects = weeks/months
4. **Mercury retrograde = caution** - Reduce trading activity
5. **Full/New moons = watch for reversals** - Especially in volatile markets
6. **Combine systems** - Western for timing, Vedic for deeper analysis
